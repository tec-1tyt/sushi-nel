About

A sakura blooms in the room. Bonsai and small lamps on the tables; at night they light only the wood and the plates.

A café-bar: wine, sparkling, beer or whisky with your rolls.

Drag sideways

01

One evening

“What if we tried making sushi ourselves?”

It didn’t start with a business plan. It started with curiosity. One evening, in an ordinary home kitchen, we simply gave it a go. The rice was sticky, the rolls fell apart, and the first nigiri looked more like an experiment than dinner. We laughed, ate everything, and tried again the next day.

02

Week after week

We watched videos, read, asked around and tried new combinations. Each roll came out a little neater, the taste a little cleaner, the hand a little steadier. We weren’t chasing the perfect shape. We wanted it to taste genuinely good.

03

The first orders

“Make some for us too, we’ll pay.”

That’s what friends and neighbours said. We couldn’t believe they meant it, but we made every portion as if our own family would eat it. People came back, told others, and the orders kept growing.

A flower of rolls

The flower on our plate is not a graphic. We cut the roll into hearts and build the sakura from them.

04

We don’t weigh the filling

Fish, cheese, avocado and sauce go in by hand, the way we’d make it for ourselves: generous, careful, from the heart. Every piece should be exactly what we’d want to get.

05

A place of our own

What began in a home kitchen grew into a place with tables, warm light and an open kitchen. The heart of it stayed the same: a small team, love for the craft and care for every detail.

06

And on we go

We keep learning, trying new things and cooking with the same joy as on that first evening. Come by, we’d love to treat you.
